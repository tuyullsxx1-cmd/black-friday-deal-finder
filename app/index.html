How we checked

  • Every price was read from the named source on 27 November 2026 — a deals roundup that names the store and the price it showed, or the retailer’s own release. The source and its date sit on every deal page.
  • The discount is computed from the two prices on the card, so the numbers always add up.
  • The verdict is honest on purpose: NEW LOW means the lowest price on record, STRONG DEAL means 40%+ off list, SOLID means a real cut that is not a record, and ROUTINE SALE means a small cut — buy it for the price, not the discount.
  • Links go to the store’s own search for the product, so the link always lands on the store with the matching product. Prices move on Black Friday — confirm at checkout.
